1. Data controller

Clonexa is operated by OSCAR NICOLAS GOMEZ, an individual registered with a tax ID (RUT), domiciled in Bogotá, Colombia, while the company Clonexa is being incorporated. Contact email: clonexasaas@gmail.com.

This policy is governed by Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (incorporated into Decree 1074 of 2015) and other Colombian data protection rules.

2. What data we collect

  • a) Clonexa platform customers: business name, name and contact details of the person in charge, the users the customer creates and the information the customer records in its modules (orders, inventory, shifts, payroll, customers, reports).
  • b) People who contact us: name, email, phone, business name and type, and the message they send us through the website, WhatsApp or social media.
  • c) People who interact with our social media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube): public name or username, comments and messages they send us, and aggregated statistics provided by those platforms.
  • d) Technical data: IP address, browser type and usage data needed for the security and operation of the service.

We do not request sensitive data or data from minors.

3. What we use it for

  • To provide, maintain and support the Clonexa service.
  • To answer requests, schedule demos and send commercial information about Clonexa to whoever asked for it or wrote to us.
  • To publish and manage our own social media content and reply to comments and messages.
  • To measure and improve our service and our posts.
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

The data our customers record in their modules belongs to the customer; Clonexa processes it only on the customer's behalf to provide the service and does not use it for its own purposes.

4. Use of artificial intelligence

Clonexa uses artificial intelligence providers to create content and support customer service. "Nexa" is a virtual presenter created with AI and identifies herself as such in our posts. Comments and messages sent to us may be processed by AI to classify them and prepare replies; a member of the Clonexa team reviews and approves what is published and any commercial messages.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with providers that help us operate, under confidentiality and security duties: cloud hosting (Railway, with servers in the United States), AI providers (such as Anthropic and HeyGen), automation tools (n8n) and the platforms where we publish (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google). Some of these providers are outside Colombia, so there may be international data transfers with the safeguards required by law. We may also hand data over to authorities when the law requires it.

6. Your rights

As a data subject you can: access, update and correct your data; request proof of your authorization; be informed of how we use it; revoke your authorization or ask us to delete it when there is no legal or contractual duty to keep it; access your data free of charge; and file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC).

7. How to exercise your rights

Write to us at clonexasaas@gmail.com with your name, the data or account your request refers to and what you are asking for.

  • Inquiries: we reply within 10 business days at most (extendable by 5 more business days, with notice to you).
  • Claims (correction, update or deletion): we reply within 15 business days at most (extendable by 8 more business days, with notice to you).

To request the deletion of your data, see also the "Delete my data" page.

8. Security and retention

We use reasonable security measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), access with username and password, data separated by company and limited permissions. We keep data for as long as the relationship or the purpose for which it was collected lasts and for the time required by legal and accounting rules; after that we delete or anonymize it.

9. Cookies

The website may use technical cookies needed to work and, if they are enabled in the future, measurement cookies. You can block them from your browser.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
