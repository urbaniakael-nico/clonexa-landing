How to request the deletion of your data

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This is a translation. If there are any differences, the Spanish version prevails (“Si hay diferencias, prevalece la versión en español.”). Read the Spanish version.

If you interacted with Clonexa or with Nexa (through the website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn or YouTube) and want us to delete your personal data, you can ask as follows:

  1. Write to clonexasaas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include: your name, the username or number you contacted us from and the network or channel you used.
  3. We will confirm that we received your request and resolve it within 15 business days at most. We will let you know by email once it is done.

What we delete: your contact details, the messages and comments stored in our tools and any record of you in our list of commercial contacts.

What we cannot delete: information the law requires us to keep (for example, billing data) or public comments you posted yourself on a social network; you can delete those yourself on that network.

If you connected your Facebook or Instagram account to our app, you can also remove its access at any time:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites) > choose "Clonexa Nexa" > Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings > Apps and websites > choose "Clonexa Nexa" > Remove.

If you are a Clonexa customer and want to delete your company's information, write to us from the email registered to your account.

More information in our Privacy policy.
